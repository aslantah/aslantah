## Selected research

The results offer little support for the proposed signal; the notebooks document the methods, findings, and limitations
- Deep Research Agents for Investment Surveillance in Information-Sparse Markets: Co-authored research at BlackRock on extracting investment signals from unstructured municipal disclosures, accepted at ACM ICAIF 2026
